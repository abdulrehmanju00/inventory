Culinary Description

Crispy chicken fillet burger with cheese, signature garlic mayo sauce and fresh artisan brioche bun. Spec requires crisp crumb coating, balanced core internal seasoning, and flash toasted inner crown/heel buns.

Preparation Method

6 Standard Stages
  1. 1
    Marinate chicken breast fillets using approved seasoning blend for minimum 45 minutes below 4°C in designated cold storage prep tray.
  2. 2
    Coat chicken fillets evenly in seasoned flour and dredge mixture, shaking off excess dust to ensure consistent crust formation.
  3. 3
    Deep fry at 175°C for approximately 6–8 minutes until the required internal temperature of 74°C (165°F) is reached.
  4. 4
    Toast brioche burger buns lightly on the flat griddle until golden brown with crisp ring edge.
  5. 5
    Assemble bun heel, 15g garlic mayonnaise, crispy chicken fillet, melted mozzarella slice, and crown bun.
  6. 6
    Perform presentation quality check, inspect wrapper integrity, and transfer immediately to service pass.
